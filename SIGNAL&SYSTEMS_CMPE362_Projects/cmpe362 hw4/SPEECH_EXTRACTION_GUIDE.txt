AVAILABLE OUTPUT FILES (listen to compare):
-------------------------------------------

1. speech_mild.wav
   • Preset: MILD
   • Alpha: 1.8 (gentle background suppression)
   • Best for: Maximum naturalness, keeps some background ambiance
   • Use when: You want clear speech but natural cafe atmosphere retained

2. speech_enhanced.wav  ⭐ RECOMMENDED
   • Preset: MODERATE
   • Alpha: 2.8 (balanced suppression)
   • Best for: Good balance of clarity and naturalness
   • Use when: Standard use case – clear speech with minimal music

3. speech_clean.wav
   • Method: Basic spectral subtraction (v2)
   • More aggressive but may sound thin
   • Preserves only 5% of original energy

4. music_filtered.wav
   • Method: Simple bandpass (300–3400 Hz)
   • Keeps ALL sound in speech band (including music)
   • Not true separation, just frequency filtering

HOW TO ADJUST:
-------------
If you want EVEN LESS background suppression:
  → Edit extract_speech_v3.m, line 18: change 'moderate' to 'mild'
  → Or use speech_mild.wav (already generated)

If you want MORE aggressive suppression:
  → Edit extract_speech_v3.m, line 18: change 'moderate' to 'aggressive'
  → Then run: matlab -batch "extract_speech_v3"

KEY IMPROVEMENTS IN V3 (speech_enhanced.wav & speech_mild.wav):
---------------------------------------------------------------
✓ Multi-segment noise estimation (beginning + end + quietest 20% of frames)
✓ Perceptual weighting (emphasizes 1-4 kHz critical speech band)
✓ Temporal smoothing (reduces "musical noise" artifacts)
✓ Spectral smoothing (prevents random frequency spikes)
✓ Gentle expander instead of hard gate (more natural dynamics)
✓ Optimized speech bandpass (250–3800 Hz vs 300–3400 Hz)
✓ High-pass filter to remove sub-bass rumble
